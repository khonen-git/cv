Projects

Financial Machine Learning Research Lab

Predictive signals · FX tick data

Independent quantitative research platform for designing and evaluating predictive hypotheses on high-frequency FX data across multiple time scales.

  • Built reproducible pipelines to inspect, clean and transform tens of millions of multi-pair FX tick observations into research-ready event and bar datasets.
  • Designed event-based sampling and feature-engineering workflows around volatility, market regimes, price dynamics and information-driven bars, with explicit prediction horizons and control samples.
  • Formulated and tested hypotheses on whether statistically defined market events contain incremental predictive information relative to random and conditionally matched baselines.
  • Evaluated classification and volatility-modelling pipelines using temporal holdouts, walk-forward analysis, purging, embargo and combinatorial purged cross-validation to reduce leakage from overlapping observations.
  • Benchmarked linear and tree-based models, XGBoost, hidden Markov regime models and GARCH-family volatility forecasts, with emphasis on stability, calibration and out-of-sample performance rather than in-sample fit.
  • Orchestrated configurable data, feature, label and model workflows with Dagster and MLflow, supported by unit, integration, performance and end-to-end tests.
  • Developed interactive diagnostics to inspect sampled events, labels, predictions and regime behaviour, enabling failed hypotheses and data issues to be identified before strategy-level backtesting.
