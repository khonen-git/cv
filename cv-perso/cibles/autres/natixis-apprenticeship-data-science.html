Financial Machine Learning Research Lab

Machine Learning · financial market data · time series

Independent Python research and experimentation platform on tens of millions of intraday FX tick observations, initially inspired by Advances in Financial Machine Learning (Marcos López de Prado).

  • Built reproducible pipelines for ingestion, quality control, transformation and analysis of large-scale multi-pair FX tick data.
  • Developed feature-engineering workflows focused on volatility, market regimes, price structure and information-driven bars.
  • Formulated and evaluated predictive problems on financial data with statistical and machine-learning models, including linear models, tree ensembles, XGBoost and HMMs.
  • Compared models against baselines and assessed out-of-sample generalisation with walk-forward analysis, purging, embargo and combinatorial purged cross-validation.
  • Implemented volatility models and time-series analyses, including GARCH-family methods, together with sampling and labelling workflows suited to financial data.
  • Orchestrated data, feature, label and model pipelines with Dagster and tracked experiments, parameters, metrics and artefacts with MLflow.
  • Built unit, integration, performance and end-to-end tests; the public framework includes around 950 automated tests.
  • Profiled CPU/GPU workloads and experimented with CUDA, cuML and GPU-accelerated XGBoost where acceleration was relevant.
